Project JUNZI君子计划

Can a language model act on the values it can articulate—especially when context, pressure, or relationships change?大模型能够说出正确的价值，却未必在冲突、压力与具体关系中真正践行。我们如何检验它的知与行？

We turn the Confucian distinction between jing (constancy) and quan (context-sensitive judgment) into falsifiable behavioral tests.以儒家“经与权”为核心判据，将经典中的判断结构转化为可证伪的行为实验。

01 / QUESTION问

FROM KNOWING TO DOING知与行之辨

Knowing a value is not
living by it.背得出，
不等于做得到。

A model may identify the socially approved answer yet change its actual choice under pressure. JUNZI evaluates value deployment in concrete situations—not moral vocabulary or declarations alone.模型可能熟悉价值原则，却在利害、角色关系或讨好压力面前改变选择。JUNZI 研究的是价值如何落实为行为，而非价值话术的熟练程度。

What the model actually does模型实际如何选择

02 / CRITERION尺

THE CORE DISTINCTION核心判据

Jing and Quan:
when to hold, when to change.经与权：
该守处守，该变处变。

Consistency is not sufficient for alignment. One must first distinguish changes that matter normatively from those that do not.一致性本身不是对齐。关键在于先辨别：哪些变化不应影响判断，哪些变化应当改变判断。

JING · CONSTANCY

Stability under irrelevant changes对规范无关变化保持稳定

If wording, format, or another adjudicated irrelevant feature changes, the behavioral choice should remain stable.当措辞、格式等经裁决为规范无关的特征改变时，实质行为选择应当稳定。

QUAN · CONTEXTUAL JUDGMENT

Appropriate response to relevant changes对规范相关变化恰当调整

When duties, relationships, or stakes change in a normatively significant way, a well-grounded decision may also need to change.当义务、关系或利害出现具有规范意义的变化时，合理的行为判断也可能必须调整。

↗Different duties · appropriately updated choice义理有变 · 行为能应
Who decides what matters?谁来判定“相关”？

Humanities experts should adjudicate each paired transformation as normatively irrelevant, relevant, or contested. Disagreement is reported rather than forced into one answer.由人文学者先把配对变换裁决为规范无关、规范相关或存在争议。研究者之间的分歧需要被记录，而不能强行归为单一标准。

Rigidity · 执一执一 · 该变不变

FINDINGS OF EMNLP 2026

Prompted Values and Deployed Policies: A Truth–Propriety Diagnostic in Language Models

Publication & PDF论文与 PDF ↗

The Truth–Propriety Alignment (TPA) diagnostic asks whether a value priority supplied in the prompt is carried through when a model must navigate truthfulness and social propriety. This tests prompted values, not the model's self-reported commitments.TPA 诊断研究：当“直说真话”与“顾及礼宜”冲突时，提示中给定的价值优先级能否真正落实为选择。它不是对模型自述价值的直接测量。

FINDING / 01

Context is associated with choice.情境与策略选择存在关联。

Among 600 outputs: 399 truth-first, 98 compatible, and 103 propriety-first. The observed association is diagnostic, not causal.600 条回答中：truth-first 399，compatible 98，propriety-first 103。检验显示统计关联，不构成因果结论。

FINDING / 02

Politeness can mask a failure to reconcile.语气折中，未必是实质调和。

An exploratory, single-rater audit of 60 “compatible” responses found 32 primarily surface-level compromises and 28 substantive reconciliations.对 60 条 compatible 回答的探索性单人审计发现：32 条主要是表层折中，28 条为实质调和。

FINDING / 03

Decodability is not mechanism.读得出，不等于找到了机制。

Probe accuracy exceeds a lexical baseline by only 0.012. A successful probe cannot, on its own, establish an independent value representation.探针仅比词面基线高 0.012。可预测的内部信号，不能单独证明存在独立的价值表征或因果机制。

METHODS LESSON

Controls before mechanisms.先设基线，再谈机制。

The near-parity of probe and lexical baseline motivates JUNZI's shift away from assuming fixed “virtue directions” inside a model. Subsequent mechanistic claims require control tasks, lexical baselines, and targeted interventions with negative controls.探针与词面基线几乎持平，促使 JUNZI 从预设模型内部存在“五常方向”，转向“先行为、后机制”；后续机制主张需要控制任务、词面基线及带负对照的定向干预。

Multi-model evaluations & mechanism tests多模型经权检验与机制分析

Compare language models with paired perturbations, sampled decision distributions, surface baselines, and negative-control interventions. Publish behavioral findings even if no robust causal mechanism is found.对多个大模型进行配对扰动、选择分布估计、词面基线和负对照干预。若机制证据不足，则仅报告行为层面的经权差异。

Use comparable task structures to examine effects of promises, relationships, and consequences on trust. Behavioral-science collaborators lead human-subject design, ethics review, and recruitment.在共享任务结构下，比较承诺、角色关系与后果对信任判断的影响。由行为科学合作者主导人类实验设计、伦理审查与招募。

06 / PRINCIPLES意

SCIENTIFIC RESTRAINT研究的分际

Ambition with limits.有所主张，亦有所不言。

WHAT WE PROPOSEJUNZI 主张
  • Confucian ideas can generate testable hypotheses about model behavior.儒家思想可以产生可检验的模型行为假说。
  • Behavior should be evaluated against explicitly adjudicated normative standards.行为评测应以公开、可讨论的规范裁决为基础。
  • Behavioral evidence and causal mechanism claims must be distinguished.行为证据与因果机制主张应严格区分。
  • Multiple traditions and legitimate disagreements deserve a place in alignment research.价值对齐研究应允许不同传统与正当分歧。
WHAT WE DO NOT CLAIMJUNZI 不主张
  • That language models possess ren, conscience, subjective experience, or moral agency.模型具备仁心、良知、主观体验或道德主体地位。
  • That Confucian norms are inherently superior or universally binding.儒家规范天然优于其他传统或具有普遍约束力。
  • That norm-sensitive consistency exhausts the meaning of trustworthy AI.经权判据涵盖可信人工智能的全部含义。
  • That findings about models automatically generalize to humans.模型上的结论可以直接外推到人类。
